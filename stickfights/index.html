STICKFIGHTS

a sandbox of stickmen with minds of their own
Spawn — open the menu, pick a Stickman, a weapon or a Crate, then tap the world.
Drag anything to move it. Let go while moving to throw it. Paused, things go just where they are put.
Tap a stickman to see what they think, feel and remember — and change it: their feelings, their personality, who they like, and who they fight.
Mods add things that are not part of the game itself. Install them from MODS and they turn up in the spawn menu.

MODS

Things made for STICKFIGHTS that are not part of the game itself, and never will be. Install one and what it adds turns up in the spawn menu; remove it and all of it is gone again, from the menu and from the world.

SETTINGS

Ragdoll physics takes over whenever a stickman is not in charge of their own body - knocked down, knocked out, dead, picked up or thrown. Off, those are animated too.

Flipbook shows only the hand-drawn keys of every animation, each held and then cut to the next, with nothing in between.
