CONTROLS

Touch

Left thumb: the joystick. Drag anywhere else to turn the camera.

ATTACK swings what you are holding. Near a rock you swing your pickaxe, near a skeleton your weapon. Hold it to keep swinging.

DASH is a quick dodge that costs stamina. SPRINT switches running on and off.

The bag on the right opens your inventory. Equip and unequip things there.

Tap the anvil in the Forge to forge. Tap the pickaxe wall by the cave mouth to look over its pickaxes. Tap Rob, or press TALK beside him, to talk.

Keyboard & mouse

WASD move · drag the mouse to turn the camera · wheel zooms

Click or F attack · Space dash · Shift sprint · E talk / use

I or Tab inventory · Esc pause

How it goes

Mine pebbles and rocks in the Cave with your Wood Pickaxe. Each one breaks into ore.

Take the ore to the anvil. Put up to three kinds in, as much of each as you like, and the one you put most of becomes the blade or the plating. More ore than it needs makes it stronger.

Rob pays $1,000 and 100 EXP for every three skeletons. $1,000 buys a Stone Pickaxe off the pickaxe wall by the cave mouth, the only thing that breaks a boulder.

Dying costs you nothing. Not an ore, not a dollar.
