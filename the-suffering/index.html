          Cheats ADMIN

          Nothing can injure you: no wounds, no bleeding, nothing cut off, no dying. You can still be knocked over, tased and handcuffed.

          You are never wanted. Officers still stop you waving a weapon in their faces.

          Cheats are only for the admin account.

          Everyone in the city sees, hears and feels for themselves, and has plenty to say about it from their own stock of lines. Connect them to Claude and they can say anything at all.

          Scripted voices

          With a key, each request is billed to it. The key is kept in this browser and sent only to the Claude API. Without one, people keep to their own lines.

          Touch

          Left thumb moves. Drag anywhere else to look. JUMP and CROUCH sit by the right thumb.

          The big button attacks. Tap it again and again for a four move combo; slide your thumb off it left, right, up or down to choose the next move yourself.

          SPAWN opens the sandbox: humans, police officers, a S.W.A.T. team, crates, wooden and steel walls, steel beams, a light bulb, a floodlight, a generator, a car, a police car, a van, rope, wire, light, medium and S.W.A.T. vests and helmets, handcuffs, a poison syringe, the M1911, the Glock-19, the AK-47, the M4A1, the AR-15, the musket, the arquebus, the blunderbuss, the bow, the crossbow, the flamethrower, the frost thrower, the taser, the knife, the machete, the sword and the crowbar. Walk up to a weapon and PICK UP, or to a vest or a helmet and PUT ON.

          ABILITIES, beside SPAWN (the admin account only): choose one and POWER uses it. Tempest Kick sends a green slash flying from your kick. Eat the Cat-Cat Fruit, Model: Leopard, for six more: Finger Pistol, Leo's Flame (five fireballs at the nearest person), Swirling Kick (a ring of air that bursts into flame), Full Leopard (run into somebody to maul them), Leopard Leap and Hybrid Transform (claws; every attack bigger, stronger and faster). A form is turned off with the same button.

          GRAB takes hold of what is in front of you: a crate, a lamp, a loose beam, a severed limb, or somebody lying on the ground out cold or dead. Your arm reaches for it and your hand closes on it. Light things are carried (ATTACK throws them); heavy things and people are dragged as you walk, as slowly as their weight makes you. GRAB again lets go.

          The musket, the arquebus and the blunderbuss are black powder, one shot each: the priming flashes, then the charge goes off in a cloud of smoke. Sometimes it only flashes in the pan; fire again. RELOAD is the whole drill: powder and ball down the muzzle, the ramrod, the pan primed. The blunderbuss throws a spread of shot.

          Armour: a light vest covers the chest and the belly; a medium one the whole torso; a S.W.A.T. vest has plates front and back that stop rifle rounds. A medium helmet comes down over the ears; a S.W.A.T. helmet has a visor that stops a few pistol rounds before it is crazed.

          Cars: walk up to a front door and DRIVE (or CARJACK, if somebody is driving it). GAS and BRAKE are the pedals (hold BRAKE once stopped to reverse), the arrows steer, HANDBRAKE locks the back wheels, EXIT gets out. In a police car HORN also works the lights and the siren. Cars dent where they are hit, as hard as they were hit; a damaged engine smokes light grey, then grey, then dark grey; a finished one catches fire and explodes. Bullets go through the doors and the glass into whoever is inside. Anyone lying in the road is driven over.

          Walls and beams are built of small cubes and come apart a cube at a time: rounds go through wood (out of the far side in a bigger hole) and through steel only from a rifle; a machete chops wood; fire burns it. A piece no longer joined to anything falls.

          Rope: TIE one thing, then TIE TO another. Drag with it, hold somebody with it, hang things on it. A blade cuts it; the heavy attack unties it.

          Wire carries a generator's power. START the generator (walk up to it), then WIRE it, and WIRE TO a light. Lights pass power on to the next. Steel wired to a running generator is live, and so is anybody you clip a live wire to: they lock rigid and cannot let go until the power stops.

          The flamethrower throws burning fuel seven metres. People catch fire and burn until they put it out or it burns out; wood and crates catch and burn away. RELOAD for a new canister.

          The frost thrower sprays freezing mist six metres. Whoever it reaches ices over part by part, stiffens and slows; iced all over they freeze solid, the skin frostbitten and the body cooling, and a limb frozen through snaps off under a hard blow. It puts out fire, and leaves ice on the street: hurry over ice and you slip. RELOAD for a new tank.

          The bow: hold the attack button to draw (DRAW shows how far), let go to loose; a long shot is sent up to drop on the crosshair, and the next arrow comes from the quiver on your back. The crossbow shoots a bolt fast and flat, then takes about three seconds to load again. Arrows and bolts stay in whoever they hit, and stick in wood and in the ground.

          The poison syringe holds one lethal dose: INJECT puts it into whoever is in reach in front of you. They are sick within seconds, down within a minute, and then their breathing fails. A needle leaves no fingerprints.

          The taser fires two probes on wires, good for seven metres. Whoever they land in is rigid on the ground for five seconds; press again while the wires are in for another five. Handcuffs go on someone who is down or has given up: select them and press CUFF. Someone on the ground is turned on to their front and their arms brought round behind them first. PICK UP beside someone in cuffs takes them off.

          HOLSTER puts away what is in your hand: the pistol on your hip, the knife in its sheath, the sword in its scabbard, the crowbar in your belt, the machete, the rifles, the muskets, the bow and the crossbow across your back. DRAW brings back the last one. A weapon out in the street frightens people; one put away does not.

          Walk into somebody and keep walking to push them back; run into them to knock them over.

          Running uses up stamina (the STAMINA bar under BLOOD); with little left you can only jog. Stand or walk to get it back.

          The police know only what they see or are told. WANTED shows how much they know about you. One star: they come to arrest you; stand still or put your hands up and you are cuffed, run or fight and you are tased. Two stars (a gun, a bow, a blade, fire or frost used on someone, a killing, an attack on an officer): they shoot, and patrol cars come after you, sirens going, and follow you if you drive. Three stars (an officer killed, or more crime while wanted): a police helicopter, either with two S.W.A.T. shooting from its doors or with four who come down ropes near you. Four stars: black S.W.A.T. vans, two riding on the sides, and S.W.A.T. on foot. Five stars: all of it, more often, and two helicopters. A helicopter can be shot down, or its pilot shot. Anyone who sees a crime, or finds a body, may ring 911; only someone who saw you do it can describe you. Officers sent to a body scan it for fingerprints, and if you touched the victim with your bare hands they match yours, you get a star and they search the city. Stay out of their sight and the stars go out one at a time. If they cuff you, tap CUFFED in the weapon bar to slip them.

          Settings has the time of day (afternoon, dusk or night) and a Cheats section: Infinite HP means nothing can injure you, and Police ignore you keeps the wanted level at nothing.

          DOCTOR lets you tap anyone to read their condition (blood, air, pulse, pain, nausea, temperature, breathing), their state (awareness, dizziness, stamina, adrenaline, poison and shock), their emotions and an X-ray of their bones and organs.

          TALK lets you say something. Whoever is close enough to hear it will answer.

          Accounts: SIGN UP with an email address (a Gmail address is fine) and a password, or play as a guest. Passwords are kept only as a salted hash; on claude.ai your account is kept privately with your Claude account for this game, anywhere else in this browser.

          MULTIPLAYER, on the title screen, when the game is open on claude.ai: a list of the servers people are hosting right now, with each one's name, how many players are on it and your ping to it. JOIN one, or HOST your own. Everyone on a server is a real person: you see each other move, fight (blows, rounds, arrows, flames, frost and powers land on the other player's own game), talk and drive. The chip at the top right (or N) lists the players with their kills, deaths and ping. The city's own people, the police, the traffic and anything spawned are still each player's own. Only the host spawns things on a server.

          The admin account alone has the special abilities and the cheats, single player too, and an ADMIN menu (or the ` key): choose anyone on your server (you are at the top), then APPLY a speed, max HP, toughness (how hard they are to knock over) and wanted level, KICK them, or BAN them for a time with a note they are shown.
