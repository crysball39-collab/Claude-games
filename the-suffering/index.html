        Everyone in the city sees, hears and feels for themselves, and has plenty to say about it from their own stock of lines. Connect them to Claude and they can say anything at all.

        Scripted voices

        With a key, each request is billed to it. The key is kept in this browser and sent only to the Claude API. Without one, TALK and the doctor's "Ask" still reach Claude where the page is opened inside Claude; everywhere else people fall back to their scripted lines.

        Touch

        Left thumb moves. Drag anywhere else to look. JUMP and CROUCH sit by the right thumb.

        The big button attacks. Tap it again and again for a four move combo; slide your thumb off it left, right, up or down to choose the next move yourself.

        SPAWN opens the sandbox: humans, crates, the M1911, the AK-47, the knife and the machete. Walk up to a weapon and PICK UP.

        DOCTOR lets you tap anyone to read their condition, their emotions and an X-ray of their bones and organs.

        TALK lets you say something. Whoever is close enough to hear it will answer.
